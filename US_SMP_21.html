The Komodo Dragon

The Komodo dragon (Varanus komodoensis) is the world's largest living lizard. It is found only on a few islands in Indonesia, including Komodo, Rinca, and Flores. Komodo dragons can grow up to 3 metres in length and weigh around 70 kilograms. They are carnivorous and hunt large prey such as deer, goats, and even water buffalo. Their saliva contains dozens of types of harmful bacteria, which help weaken their prey.

Read the following text carefully and answer questions 7–10.
🌊 Read the text
Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are underwater structures made from calcium carbonate secreted by corals. They are often called the "rainforests of the sea" because they support a wide variety of marine life. Although they cover less than 1% of the ocean floor, coral reefs are home to more than 25% of all marine species. They provide essential services such as protecting coastlines, supporting fishing industries, and attracting tourism. Unfortunately, coral reefs are under serious threat due to rising ocean temperatures, pollution, and destructive fishing practices.

A Coral reefs are a type of rock found on the ocean floor.
B Coral reefs are important ecosystems that are currently facing serious threats.

D Marine species depend only on coral reefs for their survival.

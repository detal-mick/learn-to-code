Part 2: Reading & Grammar Focus

Read the text about a young CEO’s daily routine.

Meet Alex. He is the CEO of a young tech startup. Every morning, he wakes up at 6:00 AM and checks his emails. He manages a team of 20 people. They operate from a modern office in the city. At 10:00 AM, Alex usually attends important meetings with investors. He doesn't eat a big lunch because he is busy. In the afternoon, he negotiates contracts with other companies.
Grammar Spotlight: Present Simple
We use Present Simple to talk about facts, habits, and daily routines.
  • I / You / They / We: manage, operate, check (Don't)
  • He / She / It: manages, operates, checks (Doesn't)

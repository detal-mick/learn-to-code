Short Answer — Text Analysis (3 Items)

💬 Answer in 1–3 sentences. Write in your own words.

1. What is the main difference between a Recount and a Narrative text?

0 words
💡A Recount simply retells past events in sequence without any conflict. A Narrative, however, must have a Complication (a problem) and a Resolution, and its purpose is to entertain the reader through a story.

2. Why is passive voice commonly used in Report texts?

0 words
💡Report texts focus on facts and the subject being described, not on who performs the action. Passive voice removes the personal subject and makes writing more objective and formal, which is appropriate for scientific or encyclopaedic texts.

📌 Test Day Reminders
📖Narrative = story with a Complication → always look for a conflict/problem and its resolution.
🔄Recount = no problem, just a sequence of events (First… Then… Finally…).
🖼Descriptive = describes a specific subject — one person, place, or thing.
📊Report = describes a general class/group of things (e.g. dolphins, volcanoes).
✍️Passive Voice = to be + past participle (V3) — always match the tense of the original sentence.
💡In passive sentences, by + agent is optional — it can be omitted if the doer is unknown or unimportant.

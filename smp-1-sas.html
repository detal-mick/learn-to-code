Soal 1 – 7
Recount Text
📄 Bacalah teks berikut!
Last Sunday, I went to the beach with my family. We woke up early in the morning and prepared everything we needed. My mother packed some food and drinks, while my father loaded the car. We arrived at the beach at about 9 o'clock. The beach was beautiful and not too crowded. My brother and I played in the sand and swam in the sea. We also built sandcastles and collected shells. In the afternoon, we had a picnic on the beach. We ate the food my mother had prepared. Before going home, we watched the sunset together. It was a wonderful day that I will never forget.

Percakapan / Dialog
💬 Bacalah percakapan berikut!
Reza: Hi, Dina! You look happy today. What happened?
Dina: Hi, Reza! I just got back from Yogyakarta last week. It was so much fun!
Reza: Really? What did you do there?
Dina: I visited the Prambanan Temple and the Kraton Palace. I also tried some delicious gudeg there.
Reza: Wow, that sounds amazing! How long did you stay?
Dina: I stayed for three days with my family. We also went to Malioboro Street for shopping.
Reza: I've always wanted to go there. Did you take any pictures?
Dina: Yes, I took hundreds of pictures! I felt so thrilled visiting those beautiful places.

Uraian / Essay
41

Menulis Pesan Singkat

Tulislah sebuah pesan singkat (short message) kepada temanmu bernama Ria. Isi pesannya adalah kamu akan terlambat datang ke sekolah besok karena ada janji dengan dokter gigi.

Sertakan: (1) sapaan kepada Ria, (2) alasan keterlambatan, (3) perkiraan jam datang ke sekolah, (4) penutup/tanda tangan.
Jawaban kamu 👇
42

Recount Text – Kosa Kata

Last month, I participated in an English speech contest at the district level. I spent weeks preparing my speech about environmental awareness. On the day of the contest, I felt nervous but I tried to stay calm. I spoke clearly and confidently in front of the judges and audience. After all participants finished, the judges announced the results. I was thrilled to find out that I won second place.
Berdasarkan teks di atas, jelaskan arti kata "thrilled" berdasarkan konteks teks tersebut! Gunakan bahasa Indonesia atau Inggris.
Jawaban kamu 👇
43

Recount Text – Isi Teks

Berdasarkan teks yang sama pada soal nomor 42, jawablah pertanyaan-pertanyaan berikut dengan kalimat lengkap!
a. What was the topic of the speech?
b. How did the writer feel before the contest? Why?
c. What place did the writer win?
44

Mencari Amanat / Moral Message

Andi always studied hard and never gave up even when he failed. After trying many times, he finally won the National Science Competition. His teachers and friends were very proud of him.
Apa amanat (moral message) yang dapat kita pelajari dari cerita pendek di atas? Jelaskan jawabanmu!
Jawaban kamu 👇
45

Present Continuous – Melengkapi Kalimat

Lengkapi kalimat-kalimat berikut dengan bentuk Present Continuous Tense yang tepat dari kata kerja dalam kurung!
a. "Look! The birds ______ (fly) over the garden right now."
b. "My brother ______ (not/study) in his room; he ______ (watch) TV."
c. "What ______ you ______ (do) at the moment? I ______ (read) an interesting book."
💡 Kunci Jawaban Essay No. 45:
a. are flying
b. is not studying / is watching
c. are you doing / am reading

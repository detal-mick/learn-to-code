Writing an Exposition Text

An exposition text argues one side of an issue. You pick a position and defend it with reasons and evidence. It has five parts.

Part 1
Thesis
Introduce the topic and end with your position.
Sentences 1–2 Give general background about the topic — what it is and why it matters.
Last sentence Your thesis statement — one clear, specific claim that your whole text will defend.
Example The world's forests are disappearing at an alarming rate. Every year, millions of hectares of trees are cleared for farming, logging, and urban development. Deforestation is one of the most serious environmental threats facing the planet today.
Part 2
Argument 1
Your first and strongest reason why the thesis is true.
First sentence State the argument. It should directly support your thesis.
Middle sentences Support it with evidence — a fact, statistic, or example.
Last sentence Explain how the evidence connects back to your thesis.
Example Deforestation destroys biodiversity by eliminating the natural habitats of countless species. According to the WWF, habitat loss is the leading cause of species decline worldwide. This shows that cutting down forests dismantles entire ecosystems, not just removes trees.
Part 3
Argument 2
Your second reason. Start with a linking word.
First sentence Linking word + argument. Use: Furthermore / In addition / Moreover
Evidence A different fact or statistic supporting this new point.
Example Furthermore, deforestation directly worsens climate change by reducing the Earth's capacity to absorb carbon dioxide. Tropical deforestation accounts for nearly 10% of global carbon emissions each year.
Part 4
Argument 3
Your third reason. Start with a linking word.
First sentence Linking word + argument. Use: Additionally / Beyond this / Not only that
Explanation Explain the mechanism — how and why does this happen?
Example Additionally, deforestation disrupts local water cycles, leading to droughts and floods. Tree roots absorb rainwater; when removed, land loses its ability to retain water, causing soil erosion.
Part 5
Reiteration
Restate your position and close. No new arguments here.
First sentence Rewrite your thesis in different words.
Closing sentence End with a recommendation, warning, or call to action.
Example It is evident that deforestation poses a devastating threat to the environment. Governments, industries, and individuals must commit to protecting and restoring the world's forests before the damage becomes permanent.
